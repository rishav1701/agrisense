## Decision summary

The WRMS / Telangana ADeX field-survey dataset is now a locally acquired source candidate. FAIR Forward reports >12,000 observations, six mandals, six crop types, DBF/CSV, crop health/yield/irrigation, survey collection in 2022–2024, and CC BY 4.0. Its linked ADeX ID `583e8f01-160e-4f51-bde5-31dc7f2a5887` returned HTTP 404. FAIR Forward directly links a public GitHub challenge repository and the associated Zindi challenge. The repository's `train.csv` was acquired into Git-ignored `data/audits/external/` on 2026-10-24 (3,489,700 bytes; SHA-256 `92ab358eda4416a7510d174db4925c3599678905f4883a3dccfacb228157b989`). A full CSV audit found 7,888 rows, 20 columns, no null cells or duplicate FarmIDs, 7,888 polygon WKT rows with closed rings of at least four vertices, and valid ordered SDate/HDate strings. It reports four health categories, seven crop labels, two seasons, and Telangana districts (counts below). The repository itself declares no GitHub licence; the linked Zindi challenge terms explicitly permit use and sharing of challenge data under CC-BY-SA 4.0. Apply that attribution/share-alike condition to any use of the challenge copy, and retain the FAIR Forward CC BY 4.0 description as provenance context. The file does not declare a CRS; coordinate ranges alone do not establish one, so no spatial overlap or imagery match is claimed. WKT structure/ring closure was checked, but polygon topology was not.

The TGRAC February–April 2023 point layers have now been freshly downloaded and measured against the candidate command polygons. They provide 2,446 candidate crop-labelled points, but survey date semantics, provenance, positional quality, label definitions, code-domain discrepancies, and reuse terms remain unresolved. SRSP Stage I is only an overlap lead, not a selected AOI. See the [fresh data audit](../DATA_AUDIT_REPORT.md) and [DS-09](../DS09_AOI_AUDIT.md).

The DataSuds field-survey vectors are an attractive historical georeferenced candidate: the associated article describes four Telangana dry-season field-survey polygon sets (2016–2019), crop/land-cover IDs and WFS/WMS/download distribution. On 2026-10-24, both DataSuds record URLs redirected to an IRD maintenance page. No vector file or record-level CRS/license was inspected. Treat access and reuse as unverified until the files and metadata are retrieved.

The companion DataSuds rasters are mapped rice products, not field-level labels. The associated article describes four 10 m dry-season rasters representing the number of eight classification runs that labelled each pixel rice. These may support rice extent comparison or stratification, but are derived model products and not independent ground truth; they do not provide broad crop classes or moisture-stress labels.

## Feasibility conclusions

### Crop calendar and Kc reference readiness (DS-06 / DS-07)

These references satisfy the initial source-identification step for later review. They do not select target classes, establish a Rabi 2023 calendar for the candidate AOI, or approve scientific parameters.

| Need | Identified reference | What it supports | Remaining limitation |
|---|---|---|---|
| DS-06 crop calendar | ICAR, *Kharif Agro-Advisories for Farmers 2025* ([English PDF](https://icar.org.in/sites/default/files/Circulars/ICAR-En-Kharif-Agro-Advisories-for-Farmers-2025.pdf)); its Telangana section gives sowing guidance for paddy and rainfed crops including cotton, soybean, maize, jowar, redgram and greengram. | A traceable, state-specific seasonal advisory to use as a candidate reference when matching Kharif crop/season support. | It is a 2025 Kharif advisory. It does not establish the observed dates or phenology of the TGRAC Rabi 2023 points, and it does not cover every recorded crop. Obtain a suitable crop- and season-specific source after the AOI/classes are selected. |
| DS-07 crop coefficients | FAO-56 Chapter 6, Table 12 ([official FAO table](https://www.fao.org/4/X0490E/x0490e0b.htm)). It provides standard single-crop Kc values and ranges for a number of field crops. | A traceable generic method/reference for candidate Kc inputs where the target crop and management assumptions match. | Table values are not Telangana calibration. They assume stated reference conditions and do not by themselves supply a locally verified crop calendar, stage durations, or all target classes. Keep locally calibrated values and class-specific coverage open. |

Do not build a stage lookup or water-balance production output from these references until crop class, season, calendar fit, and method assumptions are reviewed under the project decision process. FAO-56 values are generic method inputs, not validation evidence.

### Crop classification

- **Confirmed from existing retrieved evidence:** TGRAC has candidate crop labels and georeferenced points. The DS-09 audit computes 2,446 total crop counts and 264 distinct points intersecting seven inspected candidate commands; SRSP Stage I has 147 intersections. These are coverage facts, not proof of label correctness, AOI authority, independent validation, or licence permission.
- **Locally acquired crop-health candidate:** the Zindi mirror has 7,888 complete rows, seven crop labels and four health categories, with polygon WKT. It is more concrete than the previous access lead, but has no declared CRS, provider-level label protocol, verified pixel/date matching, or spatial-overlap audit. Do not equate “Stressed” with measured moisture deficit.
- **Potential historical reference:** DataSuds survey polygons may provide stronger field-footprint geometry and multi-year historical coverage. Actual CRS, data, license, class mapping and overlap remain unchecked.
- **Not a label substitute:** DataSuds rice rasters are outputs of repeated classification runs, not independent field truth. Their pixel values must not be counted as ground-truth observations.

### Moisture stress and irrigation advisory

None of the sources is established here as validated moisture-stress ground truth or an irrigation-recommendation outcome dataset. WRMS's “crop health” field may be useful as a separate target only after its codebook and field protocol are verified; disease, pest, stressed and healthy classes are not automatically equivalent to measured water stress. TGRAC's qualitative crop-condition and irrigation-source fields do not demonstrate water stress, irrigation timing/quantity, or advisory correctness. The DataSuds rice extent products identify crop extent, not crop water status.

### Spatial overlap and AOI

Only TGRAC has overlap calculations against the candidate command polygons in existing repository evidence. The ADeX and DataSuds overlap with candidate irrigation areas cannot be computed until data files or feature services with coordinates are available. No AOI is selected by this inventory.
