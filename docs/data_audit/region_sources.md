## Candidate-region notes

### Telangana (current evidence-review lead; no selected AOI)

- **Weather:** ERA5-Land is the common coarse reanalysis candidate. IMD station or gridded products may support independent checks, subject to product acquisition, completeness, and terms. No station-to-command match or historical data request has been made here.
- **Boundary:** Survey of India is a discovery route for administrative boundaries. TGRAC Agriculture2/I&CAD and NWIC supply candidate agricultural/command context described above, with material unresolved provenance and CRS/licensing issues. No final command polygon is approved.
- **Soil:** Soil Health Card is a possible soil-test source, not a proven spatial soil-storage input. Find and assess the actual sample records and required hydraulic parameters only after a pilot area and method are chosen.
- **Crop calendar:** ICAR's 2025 Kharif advisory contains Telangana-specific agronomic guidance. It does not establish the candidate 2023 field-observation dates as a crop calendar or resolve the Rabi calendar. Candidate crop timing must be matched to the selected AOI, season, and traceable crop-specific sources.
- **Irrigation:** TGRAC and NWIC candidates are not yet authoritative matched command boundaries. The 12 points tagged Wanaparthy did not intersect the seven audited TGRAC command polygons; that mismatch remains relevant if Wanaparthy is reconsidered.

### Punjab (initial candidate; no selected AOI)

- **Weather:** ERA5-Land and IMD products cover the state at their documented coarse/station support. Verify station/product availability and terms for any selected district or command.
- **Boundary:** Survey of India offers national/state administrative boundary products; these do not identify irrigation commands. No Punjab command boundary was verified in the current DS-09 work.
- **Soil:** Soil Health Card may expose sample/test information; it is not automatically a field-scale hydraulic soil layer.
- **Crop calendar:** ICAR advisories and Punjab Agricultural University/KVK materials are discovery leads. A dated source matched to the chosen crop, season, and location has not been accepted in this audit.
- **Irrigation:** Identify a project authority's command-area polygon, metadata, vintage, and reuse terms before treating an irrigation project as an AOI.

### Tamil Nadu (initial candidate; no selected AOI)

- **Weather:** ERA5-Land and IMD station/gridded products are candidates. Confirm temporal coverage and access for any selected district/command.
- **Boundary:** Survey of India provides national/state administrative boundary products. TNGIS is a state GIS discovery source, but its portal terms constrain sharing/publishing and require permission in relevant cases; check individual layer ownership and terms.
- **Soil:** Soil Health Card is a possible sample/test source, not a validated continuous hydraulic parameter surface.
- **Crop calendar:** TNAU's zone-level crop planning/cropping-pattern resources and ICAR advisories can inform evidence review. They are contextual references rather than plot-level crop phenology or planting dates.
- **Irrigation:** No selected Tamil Nadu command boundary has been validated in this inventory. Source authority, project-specific geometry, CRS, vintage, and reuse terms must be documented.

## Required evidence before source handoff

1. For every selected file or service layer, save provider, stable item/layer ID, retrieval date, source version/vintage, CRS, spatial and temporal support, native resolution, metadata, checksum where a file is acquired, and its exact reuse/attribution terms.
2. Distinguish administrative boundaries, irrigation command boundaries, field labels, soil tests, station data, and gridded reanalysis. Do not use one category as a proxy for another.
3. For weather, inspect actual candidate-area coverage and missingness, variable definitions, accumulation/mean semantics, units, time zone, and native grid before deriving ET0 or water balance.
4. For crop calendars, trace crop, variety where relevant, season, location, source date, and whether statements are recommendations, normals, or measured observations.
5. For soil inputs, establish whether the chosen method needs soil storage. If it does, obtain appropriate hydraulic parameters and initial conditions with provenance; do not substitute soil-health nutrient tests.
6. For command boundaries, verify authority, source lineage/version, CRS, topology, area semantics, and licence. Do not perform cross-source overlays while the NWIC CRS is unresolved.
