### Zindi mirror full-file audit (2026-10-24)

The raw `train.csv` is saved in Git-ignored `data/audits/external/` with its SHA-256 and parsed summary in `zindi_audit.json`. Audit results: 7,888 data rows; no null fields or duplicate FarmIDs; all rows in Telangana across 14 districts; all harvest dates on/after sowing dates; 7,888 POLYGON WKT rows whose exterior rings pass basic closure/length checks. Full category/crop/season counts are in the crop-label inventory. Coordinate bounds are x 77.488246–80.296641 and y 16.394528–19.911204; source CRS is undeclared, so do not infer one from bounds. Polygon topology, selected-AOI overlap, and pixel-date match remain unverified. Zindi challenge rules permit data use/sharing under CC-BY-SA 4.0; preserve attribution and share-alike conditions. `Stressed` remains an unvalidated crop-health class, not a measured soil-moisture label.

## Data handling decision

The audit script preserves ArcGIS JSON and derived CSV/Markdown separately under the ignored `data/audits/tgrac/` folder. Do not commit large/raw source downloads or redistribute them until reuse is confirmed. Record access timestamp, source URL, license text/version, attribution and checksum for every accepted source file.

## Source notes

- FAIR Forward's [WRMS/ADeX record](https://fair-forward.github.io/datasets/projects/ui_29-predicting_crop_health_using_opensource_geospatial/) links ADeX ID `583e8f01-160e-4f51-bde5-31dc7f2a5887` (404) and the public challenge repository. The repository has no declared GitHub licence. Zindi's linked [challenge rules](https://zindi.world/competitions/telangana-crop-health-challenge) explicitly permit use/sharing of challenge data under CC-BY-SA 4.0. The acquired CSV is held under ignored audit storage; see its hash and counts in the crop-label inventory. FAIR Forward separately describes the source dataset as CC BY 4.0. Keep both statements with attribution and apply the challenge-copy share-alike condition.
- TGRAC's [command-area monitoring description](https://tgrac.telangana.gov.in/trac/project-water.php) documents boundary methods at program level, while CWC/NWIC currently publishes a [nationwide command-area GeoJSON resource](https://nwdp.nwic.gov.in/en/dataset/water-resource-project/resource/7b1e0abf-ca24-46d2-b0dc-7234021e414d) marked “Other (Open)” and last updated 2025-11-17. The full resource is now downloaded and parsed in ignored storage; it includes a same-named Sriramsagar Stage-I feature, but CRS and specific terms remain unresolved.
- The [Data in Brief article](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12396292/) supports the stated descriptions of the DataSuds products, but is secondary to the live data records and does not establish their license. On 2026-10-24, both DataSuds record URLs redirected to the IRD maintenance page.
- Earth Engine [access requirements](https://developers.google.com/earth-engine/guides/access) and [authentication instructions](https://developers.google.com/earth-engine/guides/auth) are maintained by Google.
